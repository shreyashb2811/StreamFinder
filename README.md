StreamFinder – Career & Stream Selection Quiz
StreamFinder is a free, student-friendly career and stream selection quiz created by Shreyash Bajpai. Choose from 25, 50, or 100 questions based on your preferred level of detail. The quiz analyzes your interests, strengths, subjects, skills, personality, and career preferences to suggest suitable school streams and career paths such as Science, Commerce, Humanities, and Creative & Design.
Use StreamFinder as a starting point to explore your future and discover careers that may match your interests.
